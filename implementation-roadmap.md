### For Artisans
- **Income Increase**: Average 30% increase in artisan income

### For Platform
- **User Growth**: 5,000 active buyers within first year
- **Retention**: 70% customer retention rate
- **Product Range**: 2,000 products across 20 craft categories
- **Cultural Impact**: 100 detailed craft traditions documented

### For Sustainability
- **Eco-Score**: Average eco-score improves by 15% in first year
- **Carbon Footprint**: Measure and offset 100% of shipping emissions
- **Sustainable Materials**: 80% of products using sustainable materials
- **Waste Reduction**: Documented reduction in packaging waste
